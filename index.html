About MedCare

Healing With Heart, Powered by Science

Established in 2001, MedCare Hospital has been at the forefront of medical innovation in the region. Our 400-bed facility serves over 50,000 patients annually across 8 speciality departments.

We believe in a holistic approach — treating not just the illness but the whole person. Our multidisciplinary teams collaborate to create personalised treatment plans that lead to better outcomes and faster recovery.
